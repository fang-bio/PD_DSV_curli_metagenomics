# Main figures

This folder contains the main figures associated with the manuscript:

**Prevalence-level associations of *Desulfovibrio* and curli-associated genes with Parkinson’s disease and inferred functional potential**

Fang Chi, Yue Yin, Shuai Ji, Tommi Vatanen and Per E. J. Saris

The main figures summarize the multicohort analysis of fecal metagenomic profiles, targeted associations of microbial features with Parkinson’s disease case status, ecological correlations and joint associations involving *Desulfovibrio* and curli-associated genes, and variation in inferred metagenomic functional profiles associated with their co-elevation.

---

## Figure 1

**Multi-cohort analysis of fecal metagenomic profiles in Parkinson’s disease**

- **Panel a:** Geographic distribution of the 1,609 fecal metagenomes included in the final analyses and sample numbers per cohort after quality control.
- **Panel b:** Principal coordinates analysis (PCoA) of gut microbial community composition based on Aitchison distance in Parkinson’s disease cases and controls.
- **Panel c:** Targeted associations of *Desulfovibrio*, *Escherichia coli* and curli-associated genes with Parkinson’s disease case status, assessed using the MaAsLin3 mixed-effects framework. Detection-prevalence associations and abundance associations among samples in which the corresponding feature was detected were evaluated separately.
- **Panel d:** Complementary comparisons of the overall abundance distributions of *Desulfovibrio*, *E. coli* and curli-associated genes between Parkinson’s disease cases and controls. Relative abundances across all samples were visualized after log10 transformation with a pseudocount of 10⁻⁶. Group differences were assessed using two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests.

---

## Figure 2

**Ecological relationships and joint associations of *Desulfovibrio* and curli-associated genes with Parkinson’s disease case status**

- **Panel a:** Spearman rank correlations between *Desulfovibrio* abundance and curli-associated gene abundance among samples in which both features were detected, stratified by disease status.
- **Panel b:** Spearman rank correlations between *Desulfovibrio* abundance and total *E. coli* abundance among samples in which both features were detected, stratified by disease status.
- **Panel c:** Forest plot of odds ratios and 95% confidence intervals for the *Desulfovibrio* × curli-associated gene and *Desulfovibrio* × total *E. coli* interaction terms estimated using mixed-effects logistic regression. Continuous microbial features were standardized as z-scores before model fitting.
- **Panel d:** Predicted probability of Parkinson’s disease case status as a function of standardized *Desulfovibrio* abundance and curli-associated gene abundance, derived from the mixed-effects logistic regression model.

Spearman correlation coefficients and P values are shown for the correlation analyses. Mixed-effects logistic regression models included cohort identity as a random effect.

## Figure 3

**Variation in inferred metagenomic functional profiles and pathway-level features associated with *Desulfovibrio*–curli co-elevation**

- **Panel a:** Principal coordinates analysis (PCoA) of inferred metagenomic functional profiles based on Aitchison distance. Samples were classified into Both Low, Curli Only, *Desulfovibrio* Only and Both High groups using within-cohort median abundance thresholds. Ellipses denote 80% confidence intervals. The PERMANOVA result for the *Desulfovibrio* × curli-associated gene interaction term is shown.
- **Panel b:** Diverging lollipop plot of pathway-level interaction coefficients from multivariable interaction models. Pathways are ordered according to the interaction coefficient (β), and point size represents statistical significance as −log₁₀-transformed Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted q values.
- **Panel c:** Heatmap of group-mean standardized pathway abundances for representative pathways with positive *Desulfovibrio* × curli-associated gene interaction coefficients across the four exposure groups.
- **Panel d:** Heatmap of group-mean standardized pathway abundances for representative pathways with negative *Desulfovibrio* × curli-associated gene interaction coefficients across the four exposure groups. Heatmap colours represent group-mean pathway z-scores.

## Notes

The main figures were generated using the analysis scripts and processed data provided in this repository. They summarize the higher detection prevalence of *Desulfovibrio*, *E. coli* and curli-associated genes in Parkinson’s disease cases; disease-stratified ecological correlations; continuous and categorical joint associations involving *Desulfovibrio* and curli-associated genes; and variation in inferred pathway-level functional profiles associated with their co-elevation.

The curli-associated gene measure was derived from community-level unstratified HUMAnN gene-family profiles and should not be interpreted as exclusively *E. coli*-derived curli gene abundance. The statistical associations shown in these figures do not establish causality, disease risk, biological interaction, gene expression or microbial functional activity.
